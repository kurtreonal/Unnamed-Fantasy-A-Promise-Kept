# Updated character direction

Rin (the character previously stored under Asami assets) and the protagonist are both 17-year-old high-school classmates. The present-day campaign takes place during a four-week spring recess. The eight-years-earlier promise is a childhood promise of friendship; the protagonist's family provided Rin a home.

Rin's replacement design uses a youthful face, chestnut twin braids, and a modest collared school uniform and cardigan. Romance is gentle and non-explicit. Supporting characters such as the healer, merchant and archivist remain adults.

The latest visual direction restores the muted colored illustrations and room, with a compact status bar and small outlined panels. The black-and-white filter is no longer used. Rin sits on the sofa, anchored by her pelvis to its cushion with a small contact shadow; the old separate chair ends were removed while preserving her face. Clicking her hides the seated image and brings a much closer waist-up talking portrait into the foreground. It stays through the conversation, then the seated view returns. No stand-up animation is needed.

Cooking, reading and other activities use text and Dialogic choices. The complex cooking, reading and bed-pose illustrations remain withdrawn and archived privately. The seated overlay and half-body conversation portrait are the active home assets. The close-up keeps hands outside the frame, has complete hair margins and properly worn school clothing, and uses the existing standing design as its face and clothing reference. The original standing image remains a production reference.

The local AOM3 revision uses the original gsdf EasyNegativeV2 SafeTensors embedding, verified against its published SHA256. It supplements explicit exclusions for anatomy, duplicated limbs, framing, clothing, unwanted text and monochrome output. Negative prompts are guidance; final assets still require visual review. Prompts, seeds, hashes, sources and review notes accompany the PNGs.

This direction supersedes the earlier 22/24-year-old character designs. Earlier generated candidates stay private and are excluded from the release assets. Scene illustrations use environmental compositions where possible, while the consistent Rin portrait supplies character expression.
